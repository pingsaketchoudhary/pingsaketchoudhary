## 1. Brand Philosophy & Identity Guidelines

### What We Emphasize (World-Class Engineering)
- **High-Signal Technical Precision:** Clear, un-embellished statements of systems software capabilities in Go and Rust.
- **Scientific AI Security:** Precise terminology for AI vulnerability assessment, AST static analysis, and LLM red teaming.
- **Founder Authority:** Transparent presentation of **Cyberfact Security** as a real software entity.

### Anti-Patterns Strictly Avoided
- ❌ No fake "TOP SECRET LEVEL 5" stamps or clearance graphics.
- ❌ No Anonymous masks, skulls, or matrix green terminal text.
- ❌ No exaggerated claims or un-verified certifications.
- ❌ No changing or altering the profile avatar picture (strictly preserved).
